## What would establish an upper hand

There is no honest basis yet to claim “better in every way.” Compare on the actual buyer's work, using the same task, knowledge, customer context, channel conditions and scoring rules for every system. Pricing pages and vendor demos do not substitute for this evaluation.

| Hypothesis | Evidence we need | Failure signal |
|---|---|---|
| Better Indian English/Hinglish conversations | Blind rated recordings across accents, names, corrections, code-switching and noisy conditions | High preference rating but incorrect names, dates or commitments |
| More natural turn-taking | End-to-end response-delay distribution, interruption stop time, recovery and silence handling on representative devices | Good average latency with frequent long stalls or talking over people |
| Better relational continuity | Correct permitted recall, correction/revocation tests, context-boundary tests and repeat-interaction ratings | Warm style with stale, invented or inappropriately disclosed memory |
| Better business usefulness | Confirmed consultations, resolved service issues and completed follow-ups per eligible cohort | More calls or longer conversations without useful outcomes |
| More trustworthy company answers | Source-grounded factual accuracy, abstention when unknown, source freshness and conflict resolution | Fluent answers that contradict current approved service information |
| Better feedback operations | Completed interviews, evidence-linked themes, owner response time, improvement completion and customer follow-up | A sentiment dashboard with no action or customer-visible resolution |
| Better economics | Fully loaded cost per useful outcome: inference, voice, transport, retries and human handling | Cheap model calls that create costly corrections or failed conversations |

Sample sizes, tasks and acceptance thresholds must be agreed before testing. Do not choose thresholds after seeing results. Separate regression tests, internal listening, blinded comparison and a live pilot; they answer different questions.

## What the current prototype can establish

Whether users can understand the company-to-campaign flow; whether campaign controls are in the right places; whether the customer timeline preserves continuity; whether feedback becomes useful team work; whether the overall product deserves a paid pilot discussion.

It cannot establish live voice quality, external delivery, real extraction, representative customer demand, ROI or competitor superiority. Those require the next stage after product alignment.
