# Product experience direction — 9 October 2026

Planning judgment based on the current local implementation, `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `context/STATE.md`, and the October 9 research synthesis. This is an implementation direction, not evidence that the proposed experience already exists.

## The product promise

“Give your company a capable AI representative, prove how it should behave, and turn each conversation into accountable follow-through.”

The founder's job is to achieve a specific customer outcome with a small team. They should arrive with an intention, leave with one saved piece of work, and return to the next unresolved decision. The representative, company knowledge, channels, and evaluations serve that intention.

The first useful story is an Indian study-abroad consultancy following up with a small reviewed group after counselling: understand their next step, capture an explicitly stated obstacle, and arrange human follow-through where authorized. English/Hindi/Hinglish preferences, dates, amounts, course names, family involvement, and human handoff are concrete operating needs. Family participation never grants access to another person's record. These are configurable offering, language, evidence, and workflow choices; the core remains general.

## What is preventing coherence

The durable foundations are strong: reviewed intake, approved sources, campaign revision fences, a six-stage campaign, saved conversations, typed results, and exact-version text rehearsal. The next product increment should connect them.

Overview currently derives an agent-led journey: knowledge → agent → test/release → channel → conversations. It prioritizes active campaign runs, but does not resume saved campaign setup/drafts as first-class work. An optional public deployment can precede the founder's first private campaign. Company opens a branding/settings form, while the business model and data preparation live elsewhere. A founder must translate their goal into the system's objects.

Publication has two surfaces with different local readiness interpretations. Agent Publish uses the current readiness state; Release history selects a passing historical configuration run. Text rehearsal is a separate reviewed suite and currently does not gate publication. A passing text case is valuable proof, but that proof has no durable place in the release decision.

## One integrated flow

**Start with a goal.** Overview offers “Prepare a campaign” alongside “Continue…” for saved work. The founder writes the intended outcome and selects a reviewed offering. This creates or resumes the existing canonical campaign, preserving its ID through every phase. A campaign requires no public-channel setup to begin preparation.

**Prepare the minimum context.** Intent and Audience expose their exact missing prerequisites with a direct route and a return to this campaign. “Review the offering” and “Resolve these imported rows” are better than a generic link to Company. Saved preferences seed editable defaults; branding completion does not block internal preparation. Intake acceptance, approved knowledge, customer identity, and contact permission remain separate facts.

**Prepare the representative.** Agent & content binds an active release, or offers “Prepare an agent for this campaign.” Carry the goal, approved source selection, intended language, and return destination into the existing agent editor as a proposal. Preserve manual configuration and explicit save. The operator can see what came from the campaign and what is agent-wide.

**Prove and publish.** Agent Publish contains one evidence packet: saved version; configuration checks; required text cases and operator verdicts; separately labelled voice evidence; changes since the active release. It explains the exact blocker and links to the owning case. Publication remains explicit. The receipt records the evidence and current authority that allowed the action.

**Return to the intended work.** After publication, “Use this release in [campaign]” returns to Agent & content and updates the binding through the normal revisioned save. Public-channel setup is a secondary branch for operators who need it. Campaign Review still performs its own current preflight; publishing an agent never launches a campaign.

**Run, review, improve.** Run preserves the existing controlled execution and call continuity. Results shows recorded evidence, unresolved questions, and accountable next steps. A problematic moment can become a reviewed redacted rehearsal case; an accepted improvement creates a proposed agent revision. A fresh candidate must pass current evidence before a person publishes it. The original conversation, failed attempt, and previous release remain inspectable.

## Three advanced capabilities worth building

### 1. A source-backed launch brief

Make the existing AI brief/content assistants feel like one assistant to the campaign. It assembles an outcome, relevant audience reasons, a concise opening, and the few fields needed to determine what happened. Every substantive suggestion carries a source or is marked as an assumption needing review. Show the changed fields beside the saved draft; one explicit Apply targets the same campaign revision.

The distinctive moment: the founder sees “These people fit because…” and can inspect exact evidence, missing consent, and exceptions before any outreach. Reuse the current offering, signal, AI proposal, intake, and campaign systems. Do not add an omniscient chat sidebar or an unexplained probability of conversion.

### 2. A release that remembers its proof

Bind required reviewed text cases to the exact candidate, runtime, knowledge authority, and case-set digest. Server-side publish and rollback must use the same current gate, independent of entry point. Keep configuration, text behavior, and audio evidence distinct; an absent acoustic evaluation must never turn green because text passes. Existing agents with zero required cases explicitly show “No required text cases configured,” not “Conversation quality passed.”

The distinctive moment: changing an instruction immediately explains which proof is stale and what must be rerun. The operator can compare candidate evidence with the active version without losing failed history. Start with authoritative text-suite/release wiring; a full acoustic comparison requires its own real-media evaluation.

### 3. A conversation that improves the next one

From a result or conversation, propose an evidence-linked correction: a follow-up, a scoped memory, a company clarification, or an agent change. Show the quote, affected record, proposed change, owner, and review action. For a behavior failure, preserve the selected moment as a redacted regression case and compare the next candidate against it.

The distinctive moment: a recurring problem becomes a testable improvement with an owner, rather than disappearing into a summary. Reuse learning cards, commitments, agent revisions, and rehearsal. Anonymous/channel feedback may suggest improvements but cannot become private customer memory. GroupAI can help operators examine permitted context; it must not invent consensus or execute publication.

## Failure and recovery design

- Missing source, unsupported claim, or ambiguous identity: retain the draft and route to the exact unresolved record. Do not replace uncertainty with a guessed value.
- Changed campaign, source, runtime, case set, or release: mark only affected evidence stale, explain why, and offer a bounded recheck. The backend must reject stale authority even if the UI is old.
- Request timeout: show “Outcome not yet confirmed,” keep the request/receipt reference, and check saved state before retrying a mutation.
- Rehearsal failure or uncertain review: keep the blocking criterion and quote visible; allow correction and one explicit new attempt. Historical failures remain evidence.
- Paused run, revoked permission, or uncertain external send: lead to the owning recovery action. Never infer delivery or retry uncertain sends automatically.
- Read-only role: show the same evidence and identify the owner action required. Loading and failed fetches remain distinct from empty records.

## Implementation order and acceptance

**First:** unify publish/rollback authority and release evidence across Agent Publish and Release history. Persist the evidence receipt, expose stale/missing/failed cases, and preserve legacy releases honestly. Verify direct API attempts cannot bypass current required evidence or revive an obsolete passing configuration result.

**Second:** make Overview resume the exact saved campaign and phase; add campaign-to-agent-to-campaign handoff. Existing urgent operations keep priority. A refresh, Back navigation, failed save, and concurrent revision must preserve record identity and recoverable work. Keep the incumbent quiet visual system, one strongest next action, and explicit save boundaries.

**Third:** connect Results to the existing improvement/rehearsal path with source lineage and scoped review. Begin with one complete fictional counselling-feedback example rather than several unfinished scenario templates.

Success means a new operator can prepare, prove, publish, run, and review that one campaign without reconstructing context, copying IDs, or discovering a second hidden readiness rule. Measure task completion, recovery success, unnecessary navigation, unresolved decisions, and reviewed follow-through. Do not report conversion lift before real outcome evidence. Local flow verification remains separate from hosting, real channels, microphone/device quality, billing, and production readiness.
