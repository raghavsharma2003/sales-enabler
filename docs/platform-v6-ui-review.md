## Required bounded corrections

1. **P1 — a resolved clarification still appears as an open founder question.** In Company brain, the selected approved offering says the consultation is remote, and its current source evidence is the founder clarification “Is the consultancy offered remotely, in-person, or both?” Directly below, `Questions for the founder` still asks the same question. The UI therefore presents one fact as approved evidence and unresolved work at the same time. Remove that question from the open set when the current offering revision cites the approved answer, or mark it visibly resolved and take it out of the actionable count. This is a record-state defect, not copy polish.

2. **P1 — campaign evidence displaces the next company decision.** The offering detail embeds date filters, four metrics, a wide activity table, explanatory receipts and three caller-observation cards before the founder-question queue. On mobile this makes Company brain roughly 4,800px tall and places the unresolved-decision area near the end of the document. Keep the compact activity summary and one clear “View campaign evidence” action in the offering overview; collapse or move the detailed table and observations behind it. The founder’s unresolved questions should follow the offering evidence and campaign action, before historical results. This preserves all evidence while restoring the page’s primary job: review what the company offers and resolve consequential ambiguity.

3. **P2 — the applied plan confuses schema intent with current result state.** `Information to collect` shows an `Answer status` column whose cells say `Not collected`, even though the same campaign has a completed reviewed conversation elsewhere. The operating contract explicitly treats these as planned columns and does not claim automatic field extraction. Render them as the reviewed collection schema: a single list or a `Plan state` value such as `Planned field`. Add a short link or sentence that reviewed answers and quotes live in campaign conversations. Do not present a plan snapshot as a live result tracker.

4. **P2 — Automations breaks the shared page frame at both widths.** Its desktop title and content start flush against the sidebar boundary, and at 390px the title, section headings and disclosure sit at the screen edge while other surfaces use a readable gutter. Apply the same page container and 16px mobile inset used by Campaigns, Inbox and Team & access. This is especially visible on Android and makes an otherwise finished surface look unmounted.

## What passes

- **End-to-end continuity is findable.** Company brain exposes approved sources → offerings → signals → campaign. The reviewed plan names the exact offering revision, desired outcome, evidence required, audience basis and collection schema. The campaign run then reports `1/1 completed with evidence` and links to conversations. Offering history links back to the saved conversation and customer journey.
- **Execution language is honest.** Browser runs explicitly say an operator starts conversations and the queue does not place calls automatically. Conversation timing is labelled as browser receipt timing rather than audible or end-to-end latency. Follow-up receipts say they await staff review and do not claim a booking, transfer, message or verified customer request.
- **Learning keeps model output and human review distinct.** Each learning card shows the raw `Model:` classification, exact quoted caller evidence, destination and the operator-reviewed classification. The cards do not claim that the model classification itself became truth. The selected English preference, practical concern and no-follow-up request are understandable and attributable.
- **Natural-language agent revision is reviewable.** The saved instruction, explanation, full before/proposed values and resulting draft revision are visible. The surface correctly says that applying the proposal creates a draft and does not create a release. The audit trail is long on mobile, but it is structured and remains readable; collapsing the full prompt comparison by default would be useful later, not a release requirement for this batch.
- **Connection and access states are candid.** Inbox directs the operator to finish WhatsApp setup and shows no threads. Integrations says `0 checked`, `Account setup needed`, explains encrypted credentials and does not claim a live Meta account. Team & access explains roles, one-use expiry and manual invitation sharing. The Join screen correctly blocks a mismatched signed-in account and offers sign-out or a return to the workspace.
- **Responsive composition passes in the supplied states.** All surfaces stack without visible horizontal overflow at 375/390px. Tables, tabs, selection controls, long IDs and action groups remain inside the viewport. Primary controls have clear text labels. The long company and agent pages create scrolling cost, but only the company-decision hierarchy is severe enough to require this correction batch.
