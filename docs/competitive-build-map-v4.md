# Research to working product

Updated 22 September 2026. This is a product and implementation map, not a claim of superiority. Public documentation was researched; authenticated competitor dashboards and paid competitor calls were not tested.

## The product promise to validate

An organization connects its tools and imports imperfect material. The workspace extracts reviewable claims, reconciles conflicting sources, and builds useful company context. A business goal becomes a campaign draft with the right audience, conversation brief, evidence and channel. Conversations generate sourced feedback and follow-up proposals. Approved changes return to the company brain and customer journey.

The proposed advantage is continuity between these steps, with understandable authority and cost. Natural voice, accurate memory and useful follow-through must be evaluated together. A human voice preset, a sentiment label or a long feature list cannot demonstrate better business outcomes.

## Source technology being reused

- **html-portfolio:** Maya's Despina acoustic profile and delivery instructions; source-derived repair, address and continuity transitions; exact-quote extraction and source-authority validation; relevance ranking adapted from memory recall. Whole-word title/body reciprocal-rank fusion is currently lexical, not an embedding system. Post-call observations are not acoustic emotion diagnoses.
- **Vyakti-GroupAI:** audience-bound context and cited evidence, correction/revocation principles, internal room participation, and durable delivery semantics. Company knowledge does not automatically expose private room memory.
- **command_centre:** source import review, campaign audience decisions, individual personalization, operational status and follow-through. Unsafe immediate retries and ambiguous identity merges were deliberately not copied.

The v4 html audit refreshed every remote head and mapped 435 branch/path observations to 47 distinct selected file blobs. That is evidence of a targeted mechanism audit across branches, not runtime validation of every feature in every branch. The port ledgers identify candidates, implemented adaptations and rejected mechanisms.

## How an advantage will be demonstrated

Compare the same tasks, business facts, language, channel, tooling and disclosure across systems. Keep text-simulation, generated-audio, microphone, telephony and real customer trials separate. Evaluate task completion, factual grounding, handling corrections, requested pacing, interruption recovery, respectful address, useful follow-up, cost per completed task and actual business outcomes. Use blind human audio ratings for naturalness; transcript judgments cannot supply that result.

The evidence lab can record versioned criteria, source-cited model judgments, human observations and imported competitor trials. Imports are explicitly unverified. It reports paired coverage and missing conditions, not an unsupported victory badge. No external comparison or customer lift has yet been established.

## Cost and integration truth

Paid inference stays on the existing Azure resource. Extraction, planning, feedback, answers and evaluation have separate deployment-role settings, but currently use the proven text deployment because two alternative deployment probes returned 404. Model-catalog availability is not deployment access. Existing free Gemini supplies the explicit Maya voice profile. No dollar estimate is shown when verified deployment rates are missing.

The integration catalog has 18 available bounded check/read/signed-input adapters with differing capabilities; telephone is the only planned entry. Google and Microsoft OAuth require registered client applications. Gmail/Microsoft email sending additionally requires granted send scope, current recipient consent and an explicit send action. WhatsApp is account-check-only; it is not a complete WhatsApp conversation or campaign implementation. Local SQLite is exercised; production tenancy, hosting, scheduling, retention and recovery still need deployment work.
