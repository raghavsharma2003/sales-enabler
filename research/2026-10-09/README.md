# 9 October 2026 research synthesis

This packet refreshes Relay's product, India-voice, open-source voice, enterprise-data and owned-source evidence. It is public-document desk research plus a read-only comparison of 56 remote source heads. It does not include authenticated competitor trials, provider calls, real customer data, deployment, physical-microphone testing or acoustic benchmarks.

## Adopt now

The immediate product path is **durable intake → reviewed knowledge/audience → exact-version regression → explicit publish**.

For data intake, create a durable job before mapping and preserve its source digest, mapping revision, cursor, row outcomes and receipts. Profile columns with representative values and affected-row counts. Let deterministic checks propose mappings and let AI offer explainable suggestions, but require explicit acceptance. Separate identity matching from field reconciliation, automatically stage only safe exact cases, and route ambiguous or conflicting rows into a bounded exception queue. Apply through idempotent attempts and compare-and-set revisions so refreshes, retries and concurrent reviewers cannot silently overwrite or duplicate work. Imported records, accepted Company Brain facts and campaign eligibility remain separate reviewed states.

For conversation quality, turn a bounded saved-call moment into an operator-approved, redacted regression case. Bind it to source lineage, an immutable rubric and a case version without retaining raw selected customer values in the case. Run the case against one exact candidate agent version, require decisive human review for blocking criteria, and record the case-set digest on the publish receipt. A saved transcript can establish text behavior only; audio, latency, barge-in and interruption gates require fresh media or live-turn evidence. Publishing remains an explicit human action.

For voice evaluation, adopt provider-neutral evidence contracts: typed and source-linked outcomes; exact version/runtime bindings; named-term, number-reading, silence, noisy-line, latency and interruption scenarios; and a single reviewed release packet. Fix the known capture wording locally so an agent describes available operator-side recording accurately. Keep Azure as the only authorized paid inference path.

For owned source reuse, add UTF-8 byte accounting around authorized GroupAI/channel prompt envelopes and preserve exact source-turn attribution if GroupAI history expands. Keep raw human text separate from metadata before any relational logic. The source refresh found 56 heads, three moved and no new or missing heads; none justifies a wholesale merge.

## Evaluate behind evidence gates

- Pipecat Smart Turn v3.2 is the strongest bounded acoustic end-of-turn sidecar candidate for Hindi/Hinglish replay work. It should begin on the public gateway and must beat the frozen Azure control on premature responses without materially worsening reply onset.
- Sarvam is the first India speech/agent evaluation candidate; Smallest is the controlled challenger. Both are separate paid services, with no evidence their spend can use Azure credits.
- A constrained delivery-cue contract can make pace, emphasis and energy observable without inferring emotion. Indic Parler-TTS is a research comparison arm only and needs an Azure-hosted latency, cost and listening trial before product use.
- LiveKit is the preferred future telephony/shared-media spike, when telephone work begins. Its operational footprint and extra transcript path are premature for the current browser product.
- A configurable intake-template export may reuse ownership, examples, validation and completeness cues from the reviewed workbook pattern, while excluding private/client-specific fields and artifacts.

## Defer or reject

Do not port the older-source lexical GroupAI scorer: its frozen comparison missed the adversarial older target and selected many distractors. Do not add a shared entry word; accountable authentication is stronger. Do not enable real group channels without verified identity, event-time/consent admission, dedupe, erasure and current audience fences. Do not integrate Dialflo or Alchemic as runtimes yet; use them only as demo/research references. Do not adopt Fish Audio S2 commercially under its current license, Sesame CSM for Indian speech, Moshi as a production agent, or a whole-runtime Pipecat/LiveKit migration.

The shared worktree is implementing durable intake review/CAS/retry, explicit AI-mapping acceptance and an exception queue. A bounded private-voice capture-wording correction has 35 local focused checks passing, including eight new checks. A surrogate **text** rehearsal now exercises case → candidate → human review in backend/UI work; it is not Realtime, audio, microphone or automatic-publish evidence. Final integrated tests, build, browser review and publish-gate integration remain open. The October 2 baseline remains clean at commit `b459624` with 690 tests; no Azure-hosted run or microphone benchmark occurred in this pass.
