# Product options: a commercial wedge for the company brain

Research date: 21 September 2026. Updated for the founder's confirmed first-customer access: an Indian study-abroad consultancy helping Indian students apply to international colleges. The intended product is general, then configured for each company. Customer access is established; willingness to pay, operational pain, budget, volumes, installed systems and comparative advantage are not yet established. This is product strategy, not an implementation schedule, and is not constrained by existing code.

## Recommendation

Build a **general company operations core with configurable industry and company packs**. Use the accessible study-abroad consultancy as the first real operating environment. The core understands company knowledge, people and relationships, work in progress, policies, actions and verified outcomes; the first pack supplies the student journey, counselling practices, application requirements, intake deadlines, partner channels and commission events.

The first value proposition should be: **help every serious student take the right next step, give counsellors reliable case context, and let owners see what is blocking applications and enrolments.** The journey is enquiry → attended counselling → agreed shortlist → application readiness → submitted applications → offers/acceptance → enrolment, with visa, funding and other dependencies tracked as relevant. Prioritize whichever transition the customer's own cases show is losing the most recoverable value. Application readiness after counselling is a promising hypothesis because it uses knowledge, relationship context and repeated follow-through; it is not established as the customer's biggest problem.

Direct access outweighs a theoretical preference for another industry: it makes process observation, historical case review, real feedback and paid proof possible. The broader six-segment comparison below becomes expansion context, not a reason to abandon that advantage. At the same time, access to one consultancy validates neither the whole sector nor a universal product.

The upper hand must be demonstrated against the customer's configured incumbent stack. Meritto already markets the end-to-end study-abroad journey, documents, partner commissions and payments. ApplyBoard gives recruitment partners a free platform for profiles, applications, document submissions, status and institutional coordination. A company brain, reminders, voice and a dashboard are therefore not, by themselves, an unoccupied category. The candidate advantage is operational ease and controllable execution across the actual company's systems, with current approved information, clear ownership and outcome measurement. [Meritto study-abroad product](https://www.meritto.com/study-abroad-education-consultant-crm-software/), [ApplyBoard recruiter guide](https://assist.applyboard.com/hc/en-us/articles/29074610147853-What-is-ApplyBoard-A-Recruiter-s-Guide)

## Study-abroad evidence and value map

IDP's published journey includes counselling, applications, offers, acceptance, visa preparation and pre-departure support. Its India service describes both counsellor help and an app for course comparison, applications, uploads and tracking. This establishes a multi-stage, multi-party workflow and competing digital coverage; it does not quantify failure rates at the first customer. [IDP journey](https://www.idp.com/india/study-abroad/steps-to-study-abroad/), [IDP India](https://www.idp.com/india/)

IDP's May 2026 Emerging Futures 9 research collected more than 5,800 responses across 118 countries and regions; 78% of respondents were considering multiple destinations. This supports treating preferences as changeable and keeping previous discussions visible. It is a global vendor-run survey, not an India-only demand estimate. [Emerging Futures 9](https://careers.idp.com/news/emerging-futures-9)

The British Council's UK-focused student/parent guide explains that agent fees are typically covered by institutional commission, with some agents charging additional service fees. Consequently, the consultancy's buyer, student user, family participant and eventual revenue payer may be different parties. This is a useful model to check, not an assumption about all Indian consultants or destinations. The actual firm's contracts must determine how value is measured. [British Council student and parent guide](https://www.britishcouncil.org/sites/default/files/student_and_parent_guide_to_choosing_an_education_agent.pdf)

| Stage | Pain hypothesis to check in real cases | Useful product behavior | Evidence of value |
|---|---|---|---|
| New enquiry | Slow response, duplicate leads, weak routing, repeated questions | Combine authorized conversation history, answer approved questions, route to a suitable counsellor, arrange an appointment | Time to meaningful response; eligible enquiries that attend counselling |
| Counselling and shortlist | Goals, budget, parent concerns and destination preferences lost between staff | Prepare a case brief; capture stated preferences and unresolved questions; show recommendation sources; give the counsellor a ready next step | Less repeated intake; agreed next actions; counsellor acceptance/correction of case context |
| Application readiness | Missing, outdated or inconsistent documents; deadline confusion; unowned dependencies | Maintain per-application requirements, request only missing items, check completeness, route discrepancies to an authorized reviewer | Review-complete applications before the correct deadline; fewer avoidable resubmissions |
| Submission and decisions | Portal/email statuses differ; student repeatedly asks for updates | Reconcile verified events, show what is pending with whom, prepare accurate status updates | Less manual status work; fewer stale or contradictory case states |
| Offers and enrolment | Conditions, deposits or funding dependencies missed; student changes plans | Track offer-specific conditions and decisions, surface blockers, coordinate approved follow-up | Offer-to-enrolment progression, withdrawals/deferrals and stated reasons |
| Commercial reconciliation | Claimed placement, eligible commission and received cash conflated | Link enrolment evidence to the firm's actual partner agreement and payment status | Eligible receivables, realised commission and unresolved payment cases |

Admissions decisions, visas, affordability and destination policy remain external factors. More persistent contact does not solve an unsuitable course, unaffordable plan or adverse decision. A successful outcome can include helping a student defer or select a better option; owner dashboards should not reward pressure to enrol at any cost.

## What the primary evidence establishes

- **Phone/intake leakage exists in vendor-observed samples.** Invoca's July 2026 home-services report says 52% of callers speak to a person, 38% of digital-marketing calls are leads, and 45% of those leads convert on the call. These are distinct denominators. Calls not reaching a person are not automatically lost qualified jobs. The report is drawn from Invoca-observed customers, not every contractor. [Invoca report](https://www.invoca.com/reports/the-invoca-call-conversion-benchmarks-report-home-services-2025)
- **High-value professional services also have intake failures.** Clio's 2024 study contacted 500 law firms: 40% answered the initial phone call, 48% remained unreachable including the opportunity to return a message, and 33% responded to email. This is a dated, bounded study; it does not validate today's willingness to buy another intake tool. [Clio methodology and findings](https://www.clio.com/guides/client-intake-legal-trends/)
- **Agencies have an economic reason to care about execution, but the exact wedge remains unproved.** Productive's 2025 survey of 93 agency leaders reported revenue growth at 59% of firms and improved margins at 31%. This small vendor-sponsored sample supports investigating margin and operating friction; it does not establish that delayed handoffs caused the gap. [Productive survey](https://productive.io/reports/agency-industry-report/)
- **Staffing buyers face both productivity pressure and demand pressure.** Bullhorn's survey of more than 1,500 industry professionals identifies new-client acquisition as the leading priority and reports some firms cutting technology spend. Its automation/revenue associations are correlations, not causal experiments. A sourcing or screening agent cannot create client hiring demand. [Bullhorn GRID 2025](https://www.bullhorn.com/grid/2025-industry-trends-report/)
- **Incumbents already bundle substantial parts of the proposed vision.** ServiceTitan offers real-time booking, SMS and agent performance dashboards. - **Property and enterprise retention are occupied too.** AppFolio describes agents spanning leasing, maintenance and resident communications; its leasing agent covers calls, texts and email. Gainsight announced AI-plus-human renewal services in May 2026, including outreach, negotiation and execution. These are product claims establishing competitive direction, not independently verified outcome claims. ## What stays general and what is configured

The general core should provide useful primitives: organizations and people; relationships and permission boundaries; cases and opportunities; knowledge with sources and review dates; tasks, prerequisites and commitments; conversations across channels; actions with explicit authority; and outcome records. A company pack defines vocabulary, stages, required evidence, business policies, approved content, connected tools, escalation rules and reporting. The industry pack supplies reusable domain patterns; each company's configuration supplies its particular practice.

Example: a student has discussed two destinations, a parent has asked about funding, one application has a missing test result, another has a different deadline, and the counsellor is absent. The system produces a source-backed brief, identifies which next action is useful for which application, distinguishes student-shared from privately supplied family information, and routes the relevant decision to the responsible counsellor. It can send an approved document request or schedule a callback under the company's policy, then verify the result. It does not decide that a document is authentic merely because it was uploaded, or that an application was submitted merely because a message was sent.

This preserves the ambitious general product while giving its features concrete jobs:

1. **Company brain:** approved services, university/partner information, process rules and historical case context, with source freshness visible.
2. **Intelligent CRM:** enquiry and student records linked to multiple applications, institutions, counsellors and family participants; conversation context updates the record without silently overwriting verified facts.
3. **Command centre:** applications at risk of missing deadlines, unresolved contradictions, overloaded counsellors, follow-up exceptions and verified funnel outcomes.
4. **Communication agents:** appropriate follow-through by voice, WhatsApp or email, with company-specific knowledge and an easy human handoff.
5. **Research/support agents:** prepare source-backed answers and case summaries; answer routine status questions accurately; send changes in approved information for review before using them operationally.

Voice can create value through convenient multilingual intake, reminders, rescheduling and owner/counsellor briefings. Emotion-sensitive behavior should mean noticing explicit frustration, uncertainty or urgency, responding respectfully and escalating appropriately; do not present speculative emotion scores as customer truth. Never infer academic suitability, affordability or application eligibility from someone's voice. Compare tone adaptation with ordinary competent conversation before claiming a business advantage.

The strongest potential advantage is a **dependable case operator that fits the firm's existing work**. It could reconcile conflicting sources, maintain continuity through counsellor changes, explain each next action, respect who may see which information, and verify completion across tools. Meritto, LeadSquared and ExtraaEdge already cover many standard lead, voice, messaging and document features; none of these advantage claims should be advertised as unique until tested head-to-head. A complicated overlay that creates more administration loses even if its reasoning is stronger. [Meritto](https://www.meritto.com/), [LeadSquared student recruitment](https://www.leadsquared.com/us/education/student-recruitment-software/), [ExtraaEdge overseas CRM](https://www.extraaedge.com/industries/overseas-crm/)

Hiring remains an optional later pack. It adds a separate decision domain and is not needed to prove the first consultancy's sales-and-service value. Generality means reusable concepts and configuration, not making every business function equally deep immediately.

## Potentially neglected opportunities to investigate

These are hypotheses about gaps between systems, not claims that no competitor exists. Investigate the first four within the accessible consultancy before treating them as differentiation.

- **Cross-system application exceptions:** the university email, aggregator portal and CRM disagree, or a requirement has changed after counselling. Resolve what is known, who must confirm it and which actions should wait. Being useful across channels matters only if access and reconciliation are reliable.
- **Student/family/counsellor continuity:** keep a student's goals, a parent's financing questions and a counsellor's professional judgment related without merging them into one undifferentiated lead profile. Capture sharing preferences and distinguish fact from inference.
- **Information freshness tied to active cases:** when approved university or programme information changes, identify affected cases and prepare an evidence-backed review queue. Do not silently rewrite advice or promise outcomes from general web search.
- **Company configuration with low operating burden:** adapt to actual roles, terms, exception handling and existing tools without a new custom codebase per customer. Configuration effort and ongoing support burden are part of the product's value proposition.
- **Later adjacent packs:** specialist agencies, staffing and other advisory service businesses share multi-party cases, documents, deadlines and handoffs. Test whether those core concepts transfer before expanding; reuse should be demonstrated, not assumed.

## Economics and falsification

The important margin is after inference, telephony, human intervention, integration support and outcome disputes. Infrastructure cost alone does not establish a viable business. Retell currently advertises a broad $0.07–$0.31 per-minute voice-agent range; its table separately exposes telephony and optional add-ons. This is a current vendor rate, not a forecast of our production cost. [Retell pricing](https://www.retellai.com/pricing)

The pricing shape worth evaluating is a platform/company fee, transparent usage allowance and paid configuration where needed. Per-seat pricing may discourage broad use; unbounded flat-rate voice can destroy margin; payment only per enrolment creates attribution and cash-delay disputes. None is a final pricing decision. Contribution equals collected fees minus model/voice/channel costs, human review and support, onboarding amortization and delivery overhead. Replace each term with this customer's actual volumes and operating costs before quoting a recurring offer.

For this consultancy, distinguish four outcome levels: (1) activity such as calls and messages, (2) verified progress such as attended counselling and complete reviewed applications, (3) student outcomes such as enrolment or informed deferral, and (4) commercial outcomes such as eligible and actually received commission. One student can produce several applications; application counts cannot be treated as student conversions. Maintain separate student, application, offer and commercial-event records.

For customer ROI, measure incremental gross profit from genuinely additional eligible enrolments, plus useful cash acceleration or demonstrably avoided labor cost, less the product fee and customer effort. Do not count every assisted enrolment as caused by the system. Compare similar intake, destination, source channel and student-stage cohorts; distinguish new leads from old-lead reactivation. External policy changes, affordability, course fit and institution decisions may dominate the result. Existing employees spending less time on administration is capacity released, not automatically payroll savings.

The evidence needed to support the strategy is concrete. These are validation criteria, not an implementation timeline:

1. Inspect successful, stalled, lost and deferred cases with the consultancy. If preventable coordination failures are rare, the proposed wedge is wrong; identify a different valuable job from observed evidence.
2. Check the current stack and best-configured incumbent alternative. If ordinary configuration solves the problem at lower total cost, the proposed product lacks an advantage there.
3. Establish an economic buyer, a benefit worth paying for and an eligible measurement cohort. Enthusiasm from a friendly first customer is useful feedback, not willingness-to-pay evidence.
4. Use randomized holdouts for suitable high-volume communication workflows when feasible. For lower-volume case progression, use matched historical cases and disclose weaker causal confidence. Do not claim definitive enrolment lift from a handful of wins.
5. Predefine errors that stop autonomous action: contradictory scope, invented commitments, duplicate contacts, unauthorized concessions or incorrect completion status. Track rework and escalations alongside speed.
6. Track setup hours, ongoing human minutes per case, exception rate, collected fees and continued paid use. If contribution cannot plausibly improve as deployments repeat, decide deliberately whether the business is a managed service rather than hiding service costs in software margins.
7. Prove generality with a second company configuration. If every new company requires a different product, revise the core/pack boundary. If value repeats with little configuration and users prefer the workflow, the general-platform thesis becomes more credible.

## Two-to-three-year scenarios, explicitly speculative

**Base scenario:** generic voice, summaries, CRM updates and follow-up sequences continue to become native capabilities. The general core succeeds through well-configured operating packs that deliver measurable outcomes with low management burden. The company brain is valuable because it makes company-specific work dependable.

**Upside scenario:** repeated interventions create permissioned, customer-specific histories of obligations, successful actions and verified outcomes. The study-abroad pack demonstrates reusable case operations, and adjacent industries reuse the core while changing their domain definitions and policies. The product earns trust to coordinate sales, service and research. Revenue comes from platform fees, usage and clearly scoped services; the command centre becomes a place owners run part of their business. Defensibility comes from workflow embedment, distribution and demonstrated reliability, not merely storing more documents.

**Downside scenario:** native vendors close the gaps, buyer data stays inaccessible, and exceptions require expensive humans. The apparent software opportunity becomes a custom automation agency with weak recurring margins. Detect this through setup/support cost, low repeatability and inability to win against incumbent features, rather than waiting for model quality to improve.

No TAM is estimated here. India and the first customer type are known; a useful bottom-up market model still needs a reachable consultancy count, qualifying workflow volume, observed willingness to pay and realistic distribution economics. The case for a general product should be expanded through demonstrated reuse rather than adding unrelated industry market sizes.
