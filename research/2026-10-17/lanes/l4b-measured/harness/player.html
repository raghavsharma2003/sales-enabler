Battery player for phone demos

Put the phone on speaker next to this laptop, dial the vendor's demo, then press Start. The page listens through the laptop microphone, waits for the agent to finish each turn, plays the next caller clip through the laptop speaker, and records the whole room. Use headphones-free speakers at a normal talking volume. At the end it downloads the recording and an event log for score.py owner --layout mono --events.
