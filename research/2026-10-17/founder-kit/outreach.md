# Outreach copy for discovery interviews

Date: 2026-10-17 (round 2 edits: rules 9-10, insurance call length 30-40 minutes; round 1 kept as `outreach.round1.md`). This is for the founder to send by hand, one person at a time. Nothing here has been sent. Nobody has been contacted.

This copy books the 25-40 discovery interviews in `plan.md`. The interviews test kill criteria 1-4 in `../SYNTHESIS.md` §1.2, plus the alternates' kill criteria in §2. Section references below (§1.1, §3.1, §6, §8, §9) are to `../SYNTHESIS.md` unless they point at this file.

## 1. Rules before you send anything

These are short on purpose. Each one is here because breaking it costs either trust or legal standing.

1. **One person, one message, sent by hand.**
   - Don't use bulk tools, open-tracking pixels or attachments.
   - The CommandCentre harvest (`docs/harvest/cc-main.md`) turned open tracking off because it hurts placement on warming domains. It also moved the PDF to reply-only after an estimated 15-30% deliverability loss.
2. **Send from your own VP mailbox and LinkedIn.**
   - Never send from a `carbonsettle.com` address. CarbonSettle is an unrelated business, and its rules keep that domain off cold sends.
3. **Use company channels only.**
   - That means the contact form or role mailbox published on the company's own website, LinkedIn, or a warm intro.
   - Do not use the Principal Officer email or phone printed in the IRDAI registers. Those are regulatory contacts, not invitations.
   - `targets.csv` deliberately holds no personal names or contact details. Keep names in your own inbox and LinkedIn, not in the repo.
4. **No cold phone calls and no cold WhatsApp.**
   - Use WhatsApp only after a warm intro, or after the person has given you the number for this purpose.
   - l1 found that opt-in is required and cold WhatsApp is not viable.
5. **Say it is research. Say there is no product yet.**
   - Never imply customers, pilots, partners, funding or results you don't have. See §7 for the facts you may state.
6. **Send one follow-up, after 5 working days, then stop.**
   - In CommandCentre the founder ended multi-step follow-up sequences on 2026-07-09 (harvest). Keep that discipline here.
7. **A "no" is final.**
   - Mark it in the tracker the same day so nobody contacts that person again.
8. **Personalise the line in brackets** with the evidence from that company's `targets.csv` row. That line is what makes the message worth reading.
   - Good: "the telecaller roles you advertised in Secunderabad".
   - Bad: "your impressive growth".
9. **Round 2: rows with no website, and register-random rows** (`targets.csv` `selection = random`, and the IMF and CA rows
   marked "no website found").
   - Use only a company channel you find yourself: the company's LinkedIn page, or a warm intro.
   - For IMFs and small corporate agents the best warm route is usually the **insurer's channel manager** who looks after them.
     Ask your insurer contacts for that introduction.
   - **Never use the principal officer's phone or e-mail printed in the IRDAI register**, even when it is the only contact
     shown.
   - **Never guess a domain.** Round 2 found two more wrong-entity domains (`dgvdigital.com`, `planway.in`).
   - If no company channel turns up within 3 working days, replace the row with the next one in the seeded draw
     (`plan.md` §3.2). Log the replacement.
   - Honest evidence line for a random row: "I'm speaking to a cross-section of [IMFs / corporate agents] on IRDAI's register,
     not only the large names, because small firms' renewal work is the part I know least about." Never imply you were
     referred, or that you know anything about their business that you don't.
10. **IMFs: research only.** Make no pilot or LOI ask to an IMF until counsel has answered the IMF distance-marketing question
    (`scoring.md` §6). The discovery call itself is fine.

Placeholders: `[Name]`, `[Company]`, `[evidence line]`, `[Your name]`, `[Your title]`, `[working name]`, `[city]`, `[your number]`.
- Use the working name only if you have settled one.
- "Vyakti Platform" collides with another product (brief §0), so "a new venture" is fine.

## 2. Email

### 2a. Insurance intermediaries (renewal desks)

**Subject:** Your renewal desk: 30-40 minutes for a research call? (not a sales pitch)

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [Your name]. I'm starting a company that builds phone-calling software for Indian businesses. Before we build anything, I'm trying to understand how renewal desks at brokers, IMFs and corporate agents actually work today.
>
> I came across [Company] through [evidence line, e.g. "your motor and health renewal pages" / "the telecaller roles you advertised this month"], so I think you work renewals at real volume.
>
> Could I have 30-40 minutes this week or next? I would ask how your team handled last month's renewal list: who called, how many policies, what lapsed and why, and what tools you used. I'm not selling anything. We don't have a product to sell.
>
> In return I'll send you a short summary of what I learn from about 15 renewal teams: numbers only, no firm named.
>
> If someone else owns renewals, a pointer to the right person would help a lot.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name]
> [Your title], [working name] · [city] · [your number]
>
> If I don't hear back I'll send one reminder and then stop. If you'd rather not hear from me at all, just reply "no".

### 2b. Real estate (developers and channel partners)

**Subject:** How your team calls new enquiries: 30-minute research call?

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [Your name]. I'm starting a company that builds phone-calling software for Indian businesses, and I'm doing research before building anything.
>
> I'm trying to understand what happens to a property enquiry after it arrives from your website, ads or a portal: how fast someone calls, how many calls it takes, and how many become site visits. [Evidence line, e.g. "I saw you're hiring pre-sales callers to schedule site visits" / "your site sends enquiries into a CRM"], so I think [Company] does this at real volume.
>
> Could I have 30 minutes of your experience this week or next? I'm not selling anything. We have no product yet.
>
> I'll share an anonymised summary of what I hear from about 10 developers and channel partners, if that's useful to you.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name]
> [Your title], [working name] · [city] · [your number]
>
> One reminder at most if I don't hear back. Reply "no" and I won't write again.

### 2c. Lending (NBFCs, MFIs, collection agencies)

**Subject:** EMI reminder calling before the 2027 RBI changes: 30-minute research call?

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [Your name]. I'm starting a company that builds phone-calling software for Indian businesses, and I'm doing research before we build.
>
> I'm trying to understand how lenders and agencies run reminder calls before the EMI date and in the first 30 days overdue. I also want to understand what the RBI recovery-conduct changes due on 1 January 2027 mean for those teams in practice. [Evidence line, e.g. "I saw you're hiring a telecalling manager to follow up EMI dues" / "you run collections for NBFC clients"].
>
> Could I have 30 minutes of your experience this week or next? This is research. I'm not selling anything, and we have no product yet.
>
> I'll send you an anonymised summary of what I learn from the lenders and agencies I speak to.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name]
> [Your title], [working name] · [city] · [your number]
>
> One reminder at most. Reply "no" and I'll stop.

> Before quoting any detail of the RBI rules in a conversation, re-read the l1 lane files. SYNTHESIS §2 cites the 08:00-19:00 window, recording and 6-month retention, logs and a named grievance officer, but l3's verifier saw those details only in a secondary source because rbi.org.in was CAPTCHA-blocked. The 1 January 2027 date is safe to state. Treat the specifics as "as I understand it".

## 3. LinkedIn

- **Insurance:**
  > Hi [Name], I'm researching how brokers, corporate agents and IMFs run renewal calling today, before building anything. Not selling. 30-40 minutes of your experience would help a lot, and I'll share an anonymised summary of what 15 renewal teams told me.
- **Real estate:**
  > Hi [Name], I'm researching how developers and channel partners call website and portal enquiries and book site visits. Not selling, and nothing to sell yet. Could I ask 30 minutes of your experience? I'll share an anonymised summary.
- **Lending:**
  > Hi [Name], I'm researching how NBFCs, MFIs and agencies run EMI-reminder and early-bucket calling, and how they're preparing for RBI's 2027 recovery changes. Not selling. 30 minutes? I'll share an anonymised summary.

### 3b. Message after they accept

> Thanks for connecting, [Name].
>
> Some context: I'm starting a company that builds phone-calling software for Indian businesses. Before building, I'm talking to people who run [renewal desks / enquiry-calling teams / reminder and collection teams] to learn how the work happens today. [Evidence line.]
>
> I'd ask about real recent work, like last month's list, the numbers, what went wrong and what you use. I won't ask for opinions on a product. There's no product, and I'm not selling.
>
> Would 30 minutes this week or next work? Here's my calendar: [link]. Or tell me a time that suits you.

## 4. WhatsApp (warm intros only)

> Hi [mutual], a favour if you're comfortable with it. Do you know [Name] at [Company] well enough to introduce me? I'm doing research calls with [renewal / pre-sales / collections] teams before building a product. I'm not selling anything. A short note you can forward is below. No problem at all if it's awkward.

### 4b. Forwardable blurb (the mutual sends this)

Before building, [Your name] is doing 30-minute research calls with people who run [renewal desks / enquiry-calling teams / EMI-reminder teams] to learn how the work happens today. ## 6. After they say yes

### 6a. Booking confirmation (send the same day)

> Thanks, [Name]. Confirmed for [day, date, time] on [phone / Google Meet / Zoom].
>
> What I'll ask about: how your team worked recently, meaning [last month's renewal list / last month's enquiries / last month's reminder and early-bucket calls]. Roughly how many, who called, what happened, what it cost, and what tools you use. You can skip anything you'd rather not discuss.
>
> Two requests:
> - **Recording.** With your OK at the start of the call, I'd like to record it for my own notes only. I delete the recording once I've written them up. If you'd rather not, I'll just take notes.
> - **No customer details.** Please don't share any customer's name, phone number or policy or loan number. Numbers and examples without names are perfect.
>
> Talk soon,
> [Your name]

### 6b. Thank-you and artefact request (within 24 hours of the call)

> Thank you for your time today, [Name]. [One specific thing you learned, in their words.]
>
> You mentioned [script / disposition sheet / dialler report / due-list format]. If you're comfortable, a copy with customer details removed would help a lot. Column headers and totals are enough. I'll keep it private, use it only for this research and delete it when the research ends.
>
> [If they mentioned peers:] You also mentioned [peer firm]. If you're happy to introduce us, the forwardable note is below.
>
> I'll send the anonymised summary once I've spoken to the rest of the group, likely in [month].
>
> [Your name]

The artefact list, the redaction rules and the recordings policy are in `plan.md` §5.

## 7. What you may say about us, and what you must not

**True today, so you may say it** (sources in `../SYNTHESIS.md`):
- We are a new, early-stage venture. There is no product in market and there are no customers.
- We are researching before building. Since October 2026 we have studied regulation, costs and segments.
- We have run scripted test calls against several existing voice-bot demos, using synthetic Indian callers. We saw problems with language switching, elderly callers' pauses and long replies (l4b).
  - Say this only if asked, and only as "on our test calls". Never name a vendor as "failing" in outreach.
- If we build anything, the AI caller will always say at the start that it is an automated assistant, and it will never deny being one.

**Never say:**

| don't say | why |
|---|---|
| "We have clients", "we're piloting with ___", "trusted by ___" | Untrue. |
| "Sounds exactly like a human" or "emotional AI" | No outcome evidence (SYNTHESIS §3.1, idea 1). It is also a deception risk under TCCCPR (§6.6). |
| "Fully compliant", "TRAI approved", "DND-safe" | Counsel has not reviewed anything yet (§8 item 2). |
| Any price per minute, any "₹2/min", any guaranteed lift ("+10% renewals") | Unmeasured. The +10% in our files is a vendor case study about someone else (§1.1 item 5). |
| "Self-learning" or "self-improving" | §3.1 idea 2: needs volumes we have not verified, and over-claiming is a listed risk (§9.7). |
