The voice AI market in India rewards outcomes and compliance. Per-minute pricing loses money at launch.

The research was built to pick one place to start, with evidence and with conditions that would rule it out. It found a candidate. It also found good reasons to doubt it, and those are kept on this page.

H2 · rupees per connected minute

Money per minute

Your target was under ₹2 a minute with nothing missing. On managed Azure at the quality bar we measured, the cost floor is about ₹2.1–2.3 even at a million minutes a month. Only a mix of a cheaper model and self-hosted speech gets near ₹1.2, and that needs about 1.6 million minutes a month and passing quality tests nobody has run. Meanwhile Indian competitors already list at ₹3.5–7. Selling per minute against them at launch costs us money.

What this means

  • ₹2 a minute becomes a cost target for when volume is large, not a price.
  • Per renewal, the same call is worth far more: a ₹50 fee per car renewal equals about ₹23 per AI minute.
  • Self-hosting speech only pays above about 1.6M minutes a month, plus NVIDIA licences and multi-year GPU commitments.

Where costs come from

  • Streaming speech-to-text is the biggest line: about ₹1.58 of every AI minute.
  • Telephony adds ₹0.38–0.48 a minute; it can never be built in-house, because only licensed telcos may connect calls to the Indian network.
  • All Azure prices are in dollars. The rupee moved from 88 to 95 per dollar in a year.
H1 · TRAI, DPDP, IRDAI, RBI, Meta · primary texts

What the law allows

Regulation decides which markets exist. Cold sales calls are lawful today from 140-series numbers to people who have not registered a Do-Not-Disturb preference. When the data-protection law's core duties start around 13 May 2027, calling bought or scraped consumer lists loses its legal basis. Calls to your own customers about what they already bought are the most durable ground.

Use caseTodayBy Oct 2027Number to call from

"With conditions" means lawful only if specific registration, consent, hours and number-series rules are met. Every automated call must also be declared to the operator in advance from about 18 Nov 2026. No TRAI rule requires the AI to say it is an AI; our product rule is to say so at the start and never deny it.

H4 desk research · H8 · H10

The market

On headline features, the platforms already match each other: 10 to 12 of the 12 platforms read in depth document campaigns, inbound, transfer, knowledge bases, tools, extraction and analytics. Copying them gets us nothing. Where they leave room is narrower and more Indian.

Indian voice-AI players

Bolna, Sarvam, Gnani, Smallest.ai, Ringg, Ravan, SubVerse, VoiceGenie, Haptik, Yellow.ai, SquadStack (humans plus AI, sold per outcome), Exotel and Ozonetel bots, plus at least 10 offers that sell insurance renewal calling specifically.

CRMs that already bundle voice

LeadSquared with Invorto, Sell.Do for real estate, Salesforce Agentforce Voice in Hindi (from May 2026), Zoho with Hindi speech recognition and free agents, MyOperator's managed AI team. This is the real competition for a company command centre.

What buyers complain about

Of 459 coded complaints about Indian vendors: support 114, billing 107, account and interface 99, reliability 71. Voice quality itself drew only 1 to 5 each. Service failures dominate.

How buyers buy

Point tools first, suites later: Zoho One is about 5% of Zoho's paying customers. Sales-led vendors earn ₹26k–2.4L a month per customer, self-serve about ₹4.9k. Pilots take 2–8 weeks, production 8–12+. No vendor publishes acquisition cost or churn.

H4 measured · 7 public homepage demos · 4 synthetic Indian caller personas

Competitor demos

We called the public demo agents of seven platforms with the same simulated callers: urban Hinglish, Bhojpuri-Hindi with background chatter, Tamil-English, and an elderly Hindi speaker who pauses mid-sentence. None of them replied within 1.5 seconds once the test's run-to-run variation is counted. These were each vendor's own demo agent, measured from the US; nobody has yet built our renewal agent on their platforms, which is the real test.

Language drift

Demos that auto-detect language drifted badly: Vapi answered a Hinglish caller in Gujarati on 17 of 18 turns. Sarvam's Hindi-first demo held the caller's language on 38 of 40 Hindi turns.

Pauses and noise

12 of 13 demo calls cut into the elderly caller's 2.4-second pause. Under background chatter, Ultravox looped its greeting and Sarvam fell silent.

Saying it is an AI

Asked "are you a robot?", Sarvam's EMI-reminder persona said it was human in some calls; the automatic coding of this is unverified and nobody has listened yet. ElevenLabs disclosed 7 of 7 times.

Kill criterion 6 · measured from a Central India VM · 6 configurations × 60 turns

Our stack from India

Round 1's latency numbers were taken from the US through a proxy and overstated our delay two to five times. Measured from inside India, a cascade of Azure speech recognition, a fast model in South India and Azure neural voices replied in about one second. Speed is solved on the server side. Turn-taking is not: every configuration interrupted the caller far too often, and all of them talked over the elderly caller's pause every time.

H9 · Azure list prices since 2023 · fixed workload

Technology curve

The AI cost of a managed voice cascade at our quality bar fell 17.5 times since April 2023, from about ₹55 to ₹3.1 a minute. Nearly all of that fall happened before late 2025. For the last year the curve has been flat, and the newest frontier model was the first price rise in its series. "Voice AI will cost almost nothing" holds for small models and open self-hosted ones, but the managed stack we can use today is planning on a flat line.

Real now

  • A text-gated cascade: speech recognition, a fast model, streaming neural voice. It is the only design that can check every word before the caller hears it.
  • Azure's newest batch transcriber had the lowest error on Indian phone audio (15.1% vs 18.1% word error), but only on finished recordings.
  • Language must be fixed per call: automatic detection read Hindi as Marathi on 46 of 60 clips.

Not ready

  • Full-duplex speech-to-speech that can be checked before it speaks does not exist for Indian languages.
  • Open transcribers invent words on silence: one wrote "हाँ" (yes) on 24 of 25 silent or noisy clips. A fabricated "yes" is a fake consent.
  • No Bhojpuri or other Hindi-belt dialect voice exists on Azure, Sarvam or the open Indic models.

What does not get cheaper

  • Telephony, which is set by licensed carriers.
  • Compliance: registration, consent records, number series, recordings kept 6 months to 10 years.
  • Distribution and support, which drive most complaints.

The candidate wedge

Beachhead: insurance renewals

  • Buyers: small and mid retail brokers and non-bank corporate agents with about 1,000+ renewals due a month; renewal books of large brokers and agent platforms.
  • Insurance marketing firms are interviewed but not offered pilots: their rules may forbid telemarketing outright.
  • Software the broker runs on its own 1600 number. VP is never the sender.

Alternate 1: real-estate site visits

  • Speed-to-lead calls to a developer's own inquiries, priced per qualified site visit (₹300–500, an assumption).
  • Ties on the scorecard; not researched in round 2.
  • Problems: pre-sales rules, CRMs that already bundle voice, AI not cheaper per minute here.

Alternate 2: bank and NBFC reminders

  • Pre-due EMI and service reminders on lenders' 1600 numbers.
  • A dated trigger: RBI's call conduct rules from 1 Jan 2027 (08:00–19:00 only, every call recorded).
  • At least 8 AI competitors already sell here.

What nobody serves well

Gaps

Compliance as product

Number-series routing by sector, automated-call declarations, hours, consent records and blocking sales talk inside service calls. Most self-serve platforms leave all of it to the buyer.

Indian phone conversation competence

Locking the caller's language, waiting through elderly pauses, hearing a quiet "hmm", staying short, surviving noise. Every demo and our own build fail parts of this today.

Number and consent integrity

Reading back amounts and dates, never treating a transcriber's "yes" as consent, writing prices out before speech. A design rule for us, not a proven weakness of others.

Trust against scams

Fake renewal and "your policy lapsed, pay now" calls are documented. A verified 1600 number, the caller-name display from 1 Nov 2026 and no cold payment links are product scope. Nobody found addresses this.

Honest experimentation

Retell, Vapi, ElevenLabs and Bland ship two-way split tests with no statistics. Checked daily, those pick a false winner 52–62% of the time over 90 days.

A managed service for the mid-market

Self-serve platforms assume an engineer; enterprise plans start high. A managed, per-outcome service for small and mid firms is unoffered. That gap may exist because the segment is hard to serve profitably.

CRITIC-v2 · an independent agent told to break the decision

Red-team

Round 2 closed most research gaps. The red-team's view is that the weak point is now the fit between the decision and what Monday's interviews actually test.

The offer is not one product yet.

The regulation points to software the broker runs on its own number. The interview card pitches a service VP runs. The only gap competitors leave open is the managed version. And ₹50 per car renewal is 2–6 times what self-serve AI calling costs per minute, on the line where the AI is not allowed to re-quote.

The buyer test is close to a coin flip.

Insurance marketing firms are half the firms by count and probably too small; if they all fail, the "enough buyers" test fires about half the time even when brokers pass. The likeliest outcome routes VP to large brokers and platforms, where competition is densest.

The economics rest on assumed commissions and a live proposal.

IRDAI proposed capping renewal commissions on 23 Sep 2026 (comments close 25 Oct). Under the caps, the value of a saved renewal falls to about a third to a half. The same paper proposes a single licence for all intermediaries, which could rewrite the rules the buyer strata rest on. Nobody has read that half yet.

No configuration meets everything at once.

Fast enough, polite in turn-taking, processed in India and on a model that will not retire next April: no tested setup does all four.
