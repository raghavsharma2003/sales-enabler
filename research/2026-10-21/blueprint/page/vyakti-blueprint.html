The product on one page

You talk. VP's agents plan and draft. Code engines enforce the rules no agent can override. The voice runtime makes the call on your own number. Every call flows back as analysis, alerts and proposals. Anything that reaches a person, spends money or changes a policy waits for your one-tap approval (the seal).

Solid lines are actions. Dashed lines are information coming back. Reading guide: the dial gate checks window, consent, suppression and caller ID before every ring; the reply door checks every sentence before she says it (disclosure, never denying she is an AI, crisis lines, numbers only from data, no OTP or payment asks).

The AI workforce that does the work

Agents (models) understand, plan, draft and explain. Engines (code) enforce and execute. Every agent acts only through the same tools a developer gets, and how much it may do on its own is fixed in code.

A0Suggests only
A1Drafts and runs simulations inside a test budget. Never reaches a real person or spends money.
A2A card with the diff, cost and risk. You hold for 1.2 s; the approval is bound to that exact change.
A3Acts at once, but only to lower risk or spend: pause, suppress after an opt-out, stop at a cap, revert to the last good version. Then tells you, with undo.

        ८

        Omni-channel data and memory

        Any data you bring becomes something an agent can act on. Three stores never mix: company knowledge, the customer record from your systems, and what the customer said in their own words.

        Sources · what VP takes · how it is used
        →
        Company brainFacts from your website and documents, approved span by span. Customer rows never enter it.
        Customer recordMoney, dates and IDs from your systems. Each value has an owner and a maximum age, so paid customers are not called.
        Customer memoryThe caller's exact words, with a link to the recording. Every fact can be played, edited or forgotten.
        →
        • Before the call: a 1,200-byte brief: language, pace, aap or tum, best time, promises made
        • During the call: she speaks the system's amount, never the caller's guess, and flags a conflict
        • After the call: outcome, follow-up on WhatsApp or SMS, write-back to your CRM
        • Next time: preferences apply from call 2, with undo

          Architecture, top to bottom

          A TypeScript control plane and a Python voice runtime in Azure Central India, with all data stored in India. Agents plan; engines in code enforce.

          १२

          Roadmap

          One real journey first (v0.5), then parity made better (v1), then memory and proven improvement (v2), then scale and lower cost (v3). Dates are target windows, not commitments.

            Learn from everyone, always

            The best pattern from each rival, adopted on purpose. A Scout agent keeps watching them, so this list grows instead of going stale.

            How VP keeps learning from competitors

            • Scout watches 67 baselined pages across the 8 deep-studied vendors plus Pipecat, LiveKit, Ultravox, Ringg, Smallest, Gupshup, Plivo and Bland: status daily; prices, changelogs, docs and SDKs every Monday 06:00 IST.
            • Tripwires fire on what would change our plan: an India region, INR billing, DLT or A2P support, a Hinglish mode, memory going GA, spend caps, a price at or under ₹4 all-in, a self-serve SquadStack.
            • Measured, not read: the same call battery runs on rivals every quarter and on any tripwire; a person re-walks their dashboards monthly on our own trial accounts.
            • Stored, never re-derived: every change becomes a graded entry in the project graph, with a weekly Scout card and a monthly digest for you.
